# Every setting: where it's stored, where it's read, what changes on screen

Research for [#1339](https://github.com/simonoppowa/OpenNutriTracker/issues/1339), part of the settings map [#1338](https://github.com/simonoppowa/OpenNutriTracker/issues/1338). Traced on `origin/develop` at `05fcce7e`, 2026-10-11. `lib/features/settings` is identical between `v2.4.0` (the build on the Pixel) and develop. Of the consumer files cited here, four differ between the two: `trends_bloc.dart`, `weight_history_screen.dart`, `intake_data_source.dart` and `meal_info_button.dart`. None of those changes touches a setting. They are a DST day-count fix (#1207), a recent-intake ordering change and an info-button guard. Line numbers are develop's. Only the Trends lines can shift, by at most 6.

## Answer

**No config value is lost on a cold restart. Every field the Settings screen writes reaches Hive and reads back after the boxes are closed and reopened, including the writes nobody awaits. What does lose input is three goal editors that drop a number typed into their text field unless the keyboard's Done key is pressed before OK, plus Nutrient goals, which saves nothing at all on a day with no diary entry yet.** Those are the "doesn't save" a user can hit on 2.4.0, and the run should test them first.

What was checked, and how:

- **Write chain, every config field.** Each Settings write goes `SettingsBloc` → `AddConfigUsecase` → `ConfigRepository` → `ConfigDataSource._update` (`lib/core/data/data_source/config_data_source.dart:133`). That reads the merged config, mutates it and `put`s a detached copy into both Hive boxes (`:95-102`). `AddConfigUsecase`, `ConfigRepository` and `ConfigDataSource` await every call they make.
- **The unawaited half.** 15 of `SettingsBloc`'s setters (`settings_bloc.dart:96-165`) are `void` and fire the usecase without awaiting it. 14 of them have a UI caller, and the Settings screen adds `LoadSettingsEvent` straight after. This is safe for reading back. In hive_ce 2.20.0, `put` applies the new value to the in-memory keystore *synchronously*, before its first `await` (`box_impl.dart:81-92`, `keystore.beginTransaction`). Disk I/O comes after. So the next `getConfig()` already sees the new value. The open risks are narrower. A failed disk write would roll the keystore back and surface only as an unhandled zone error, never in the UI. A process killed within milliseconds of the tap would lose the write.
- **A throwaway probe test** (not committed) fired every Settings write on a real on-disk Hive box, the void ones without `await`, exactly as `SettingsBloc` does. Then it closed and reopened both boxes and read them back through `ConfigRepository.getConfig()`, the same call `main.dart:93` makes at startup. All 27 fields came back: theme, the three unit fields, kJ, the raw-units default, three display toggles, reminder on and time, locale, accent and Material You, kcal adjustment, the macro split, meal shares, nutrient visibility, day boundary, food sources, health import and multiplier, crash-report consent. The `toJson`/`fromJson` round trip in `_readMerged` is lossless for every field. The test does not simulate a hard kill mid-write, because `Hive.close()` drains pending writes first.
- **A second probe** (a widget test, not committed) pumped `KcalAdjustmentDialog` and `NutrientGoalsScreen` with fakes. It typed into the text field and tapped the save button without an IME action. **Kcal adjustment saved `0` after `300` was typed**; with `receiveAction(done)` first it saved `300`. **Nutrient goals saved `fibreGoal: null` after `42` was typed.**
- **No startup read uses a different key from its write.** `main.dart:93-135` reads theme, locale, kJ, Material You, accent, reminder and consent from the same `ConfigDBO` fields the setters write. The only setting with two stores is **language** (Hive plus Android's per-app locale), and that one can be overridden at startup. See suspect A6.

Rows: **45** (numbered 1-44, plus 7b). 37 cover the Settings rows and the controls inside the screens and dialogs they open, 5 are the About group, and 3 are dialogs that live in `settings/presentation/widgets` but open from elsewhere: the water goal on the *Du* tab, the Health Connect disclosure and the AI consent screen.

## How to read the tables

- **Write**: the call chain down to the store, with the Hive field in brackets. Every config field lands in both boxes through `_writeBoth` (`config_data_source.dart:95`). *Device-wide* fields are read back from the shared `AppConfigBox`. *Per-profile* fields (kcal adjustment, macro split, meal shares, water goal, the health fields) are read only from the active profile's `ConfigBox` (`config_data_source.dart:52-80`).
- **Awaited?** means: is every Future in the chain awaited before the UI treats the value as saved, by closing the dialog, reloading the Settings state or flipping the switch? "No (memory-safe)" means unawaited but visible in memory at once, as described above.
- **Reload**: which bloc has to re-read for the effect to show, and whether the Settings code adds that event. `HomeBloc`, `DiaryBloc`, `CalendarDayBloc`, `ProfileBloc`, `TrendsBloc` and `SettingsBloc` are lazy singletons (`locator.dart:218-283`), and the four tabs sit in an `IndexedStack` (`main_screen.dart:129`), so switching tabs reloads nothing. Home and Diary reload on app **resume** (`home_page.dart:122-127,532`, `diary_page.dart:87-92,361-369`). Trends and Profile never do: Trends loads once in `initState` (`trends_page.dart:35-37`).
- **Id**: a `Semantics(identifier:)` to tap by, or "text" (German label in quotes, as the Pixel shows it). Dialog OK and Cancel are always text: "OK" and "ABBRECHEN".
- File paths are shortened. `settings_screen.dart` is `lib/features/settings/settings_screen.dart`, and widget files are under `lib/features/settings/presentation/widgets/`.

## A. Persistence suspects

Ordered by how likely a user is to hit them.

1. **Kcal adjustment drops a typed value.** `kcal_adjustment_dialog.dart:114-125`: `_save` writes `_kcalAdjustment`, which only the slider and `_applyKcalInput` (`:75-95`) update. `_applyKcalInput` runs on the keyboard's Done (`:260-261`), not on OK. Probe-confirmed: typed 300 → saved 0. The macro split had the identical bug and #525 fixed it (`macro_split_dialog.dart:259`), but kcal adjustment never got that fix. **Real bug, not yet filed.**
2. **Nutrient goals saves nothing on a day with no entry yet.** `tracked_day_data_source.dart:244-245` returns silently without a row, and nothing in `_save` creates one (`settings_bloc.dart:303` only updates). The screen pops as if saved; reopening shows the defaults. **Real bug.** It is separate from #1316, which is about the *next* day.
3. **Nutrient goals drops typed values** (`nutrient_goals_screen.dart:228-253` vs `_applyText` at `:465`, wired only to `onSubmitted` / `onEditingComplete` at `:546-547`). Probe-confirmed. **Real bug.**
4. **Nutrient goals Reset doesn't reset.** `_resetAll` (`:212`) nulls every field, and `updateDayNutrientGoals` skips nulls (`tracked_day_data_source.dart:246-275`), so the old goals stay stored.
5. **Water goal drops a typed value** (`water_goal_dialog.dart:81` vs `:70-79`), the same pattern as 1. Traced from code.
6. **Language can be overridden at startup by Android's per-app locale.** Two stores hold it: Hive `selectedLocale` and `LocaleManager`. At cold start a system override wins and is copied into Hive (`app_locale_sync.dart:27-30`). If the in-app write to the OS fails, the failure is swallowed (`app_locale_service.dart:41-44`) and the old OS value wins at the next launch. In the other direction, choosing "System default" in *Android's* picker is ignored while the app runs (`main.dart:248`: a null tag returns early) and **re-pushed** at the next launch (`app_locale_sync.dart:32`). A user changing the language from Android Settings sees it revert.
7. **Unawaited config writes**: the 14 `void` bloc setters with a UI caller (`settings_bloc.dart:96-165`), plus `settings_screen.dart:1289` and `accent_colour_screen.dart:33-34,43,46`. All are visible in memory at once and reach disk (probe), so they explain no restart loss. They do mean a failed write rolls back silently, and a kill within milliseconds of the tap could lose one. This is the #541 shape in a much weaker form: #542 added the awaits in `AddConfigUsecase`, but the bloc layer above it still fires and forgets.
8. **Health import switch UI and store can disagree** after an exception past `health_sync_screen.dart:174`: the catch at `:187-190` shows off while the store says on.
9. **Calorie profile (non-binary) is written before OK** (`kcal_adjustment_dialog.dart:109`). Cancel doesn't undo it. The reverse of "doesn't save", but the same confusion.
10. **Per-profile fields.** Kcal adjustment, macro split, meal shares, water goal and the health settings are read only from the active profile's box (`config_data_source.dart:52-80`). A profile switch (onboarding cancel, demo) changes them. On a single-profile install this is invisible.

Context rather than a bug: `android:allowBackup="false"` (`AndroidManifest.xml:53`), and settings are not part of the export (row 33). A reinstall or a new phone starts every setting from defaults, which a user could report as "settings don't save".

## B. Effect suspects: consumers the Settings code doesn't reload

1. **Show meal macros → Diary.** `settings_screen.dart:356-360` reloads Home only. The Diary reads `showMealMacros` from `DiaryBloc` (`diary_bloc.dart:28` → `diary_page.dart:77` → `day_info_widget.dart:306…`). The Diary keeps showing macros until resume. Row 12, the toggle just above it, adds `LoadDiaryYearEvent` for exactly this reason (`:344-348`).
2. **Nutrient visibility → Diary nutrient panel.** `nutrient_visibility_screen.dart:42-46` reloads nothing, and `DailyNutrientPanel` refetches visibility only when `intakes` or `selectedDay` change (`daily_nutrient_panel.dart:142-145`). A hidden nutrient stays visible until the day changes, food is added, or the app resumes.
3. **Kcal adjustment / macro split → Diary and Trends.** Both rewrite today's tracked-day goals (`settings_bloc.dart:293-314`) but reload only Home and Settings. `CalendarDayBloc` (`calendar_day_bloc.dart:111`), `DiaryBloc` and `TrendsBloc` (`trends_page.dart:268`) keep the old goal. The Diary catches up on resume; Trends loads once (`trends_page.dart:35-37`) and stays stale until a range chip is tapped.
4. **Energy unit → Trends.** The calorie chart plots raw kcal (`trends_page.dart:267`) with an unconverted tooltip (`:299`). In kJ mode, Trends values don't change. This isn't covered by #1315, which is the Home meal headers.
5. **Day boundary → Diary today-marker.** `diary_day_boundary_dialog.dart:59-60` refreshes Home and CalendarDay but not `DiaryBloc` (`diary_bloc.dart:34-36`). Resume fixes it.
6. **Nutrient goals and goal updates use `DateTime.now()`, not the logical day** (`nutrient_goals_screen.dart:233,235`; `kcal_adjustment_dialog.dart:122`; `macro_split_dialog.dart:275`). With a day boundary of, say, 04:00, an edit at 02:00 lands on the calendar date while the Diary shows the logical previous day. Combined with A2, it is usually a silent no-op.
7. **Import app data → Trends, Profile, Recipes** (`export_import_dialog.dart:160-164`): imported weight-log entries and recipes don't show on those tabs until a restart or a reopen.
8. **Language → the scheduled reminder** keeps its old-language title until the next cold start (`main.dart:109-128` is the only re-localising reschedule).
9. **Crash reports on** takes effect only at the next launch, and only in release builds (`main.dart:139`).
10. **Possible scroll jump in the embedded list (hypothesis).** Every toggle adds `LoadSettingsEvent`, which first emits `SettingsLoadingState`. The embedded Settings column on *Du* is then replaced by a spinner for at least a frame (`settings_screen.dart:159-160`), and the outer scroll position may clamp. Watch whether *Du* jumps after a toggle; a jump can make a toggle look as if it didn't take.

## What the run should do first

- For rows 7, 10 and 42, **type the value and tap OK without pressing Done**, then reopen. Then repeat with the slider, as the control.
- For row 10, do it **before logging anything that day**, then again after logging one food.
- For row 13, toggle it and go **straight to the Diary** without leaving the app. For row 15, untick a nutrient and go straight to the Diary's nutrient panel.
- For row 20, change the language once in-app and once from **Android Settings → Apps → OpenNutriTracker → Language**, including "System default", each followed by a cold restart.
- Everything else: change it, check the effect listed above, force-stop, relaunch, and check that the value and the effect are still there.
